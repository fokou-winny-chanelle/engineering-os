# Screen 3: Landing page for a savings-group app ("Njangi")

**Product.** Njangi lets a group of friends, colleagues or a market association run a rotating savings group
(tontine/njangi): members contribute each month, one member receives the pot in turn, everyone sees who paid.

**Audience.** Members and organisers in Cameroon, 20 to 55, mostly on phones; organisers care about trust
and transparency, members about simplicity. French and English versions (design the French one; make sure
English fits).

**The page.**
- Hero: what it is, for whom, one primary action (download / join with an invite code).
- How it works in 3 steps (create a group, contribute by mobile money, receive your turn).
- Trust: how money is handled (operator escrow wording as a placeholder: "[partenaire agréé]"), who sees what.
- A short testimonial area with placeholders (no invented names or quotes: use "[témoignage]").
- Pricing: placeholders only ("[prix]"), no invented numbers.
- FAQ (4 questions) and footer.

**Widths:** phone 360 px and desktop 1280 px. Light; dark optional.

**Constraints.** Must not look like a generic AI landing page. No stock photos, no real logos, free-licence
fonts only. No external images (CSS/SVG drawing is fine).
